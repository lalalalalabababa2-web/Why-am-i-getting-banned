HOW TO PLAY

Move — drag anywhere on the left half (floating stick).
Look — drag on the right half of the screen.
Fire — auto-locks the nearest target in front of you. Tap the weapon box to switch.
Car button — get in the nearest car (or pull the driver out). In a car it gets you out.
Driving — stick steers (push it up to drive too), big arrow = gas, down arrow = brake/reverse, P = handbrake drift.
Wanted — crimes bring the police. Break line of sight and stay out of the search area, or hit a Quick Coat respray.
Missions — glowing pink markers on the map start story missions. Yellow = shops, cyan = respray, green = safehouse (save).
Extras — 20 hidden neon shells, 6 stunt jumps, street races. Dead folks sometimes drop cash.
